## The inputs

- **What each paper's input is:** md-v1 Markdown (`scripts/postslr_convert.py`, the unchanged md-v1
  core). For the 55 papers with figures, the frozen figure descriptions are inserted.
- **The describer:** figure-describe-v1, claude-opus-5-5, which is not a rater. 519 figures were
  described, all returned by claude-opus-5-5, at a cost of US$5.90.
- **Per-paper hashes:** the PDF hash and the hash of the exact text each rater receives are listed in
  [INPUTS_POSTSLR.json](INPUTS_POSTSLR.json).
- **Manifest and ledger hashes:**
  - conversion manifest [MARKDOWN_MANIFEST.json](MARKDOWN_MANIFEST.json):
    `4837e682aa1d8f5b6d6c9ee4aa48ccb8d485d1a803dd50d2bcf9c26ee0afc355`;
  - figure-description ledger (519 rows, private): `ae88134f544f1355b9a3cfb9299c8fdc89e2ead161ac0d8a40d10217f12abf86`.
- **Conversion review:** 12 papers were flagged and inspected, and none was excluded or hand-patched
  ([CONVERSION_REVIEW.md](CONVERSION_REVIEW.md)).

## Exposure (disclosed per the pre-registration)

**No rater model has seen any of these papers**, and no rubric text was written from them. Three other
contacts came before the run:

1. **Screening:**
   - the analyst (claude-opus-5-5) read the titles and abstracts;
   - four claude-opus-5-5 subagents read the full texts, against the SLR criteria only;
   - no rubric, pillar or score was involved, and none of these is one of the raters (gpt-6.1-sol,
     glm-5.3, claude-sonnet-5-5).
2. **Conversion checks:** for the 12 flagged papers, the analyst compared tokens between the conversion
   and the reference text. This was not a reading for content.
3. **Figure descriptions:** the frozen describer (claude-opus-5-5) saw each figure image and its caption.

## Deviations so far (dated; to be reported with the results)

1. **1 Nov 2026, search:**
   - **Google Scholar was not run.** Its first request returned a bot-check page.
   - **OpenAlex was added as a supplementary source, and SciSpace was run only in part**
     ([SEARCH_LOG.md](SEARCH_LOG.md)).
2. **1–10 Nov 2026, screening:** the analyst screened and Gabriel checked each decision. This is the
   protocol's alternative ("You screen, I check").
3. **10 Nov 2026, full texts:** 10 full texts were not retrieved, and they were dropped by Gabriel's
   ruling (FULLTEXT_LOG.md).
   - **Four have no full text anywhere:** an abstract, a dataset record and a title-only record.
   - **Four are SPIE papers,** which Griffith does not subscribe to.
   - **Two sites were unreachable.**
4. **Timing:** stage 1 was not public before the search, or before the full-text decisions became final.
   Both stages are to be published together, before the freeze and the first rated request.
5. **Cost estimate:** the pre-registration estimated about US$25 for 20 papers. For 57 papers, at
   CORPUS3's rate of about US$0.93 per paper for both arms with discussion, the estimate is about US$53.
   It remains an estimate, not a limit.
